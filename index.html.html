Our Guiding Purpose

Vision & Mission of Vidya Shakthi Foundation

Dedicated to uplifting lives across Sri Lanka through education, financial empowerment, positive mindsets, and community well-being.

OUR VISION

“Creating a future in Sri Lanka where every person experiences both beauty and financial freedom, fostering a society where people thrive and contribute to the well-being of their communities.”

OUR MISSION

“We strive to empower the lives of future generations of Sri Lanka through rich education and develop positive attitudes, acquire knowledge and skills for financial success, but also create a community that develops mindsets and values that contribute to beauty and fulfillment.”

What We Stand For

Four Pillars of Our Foundation

Translating our Vision and Mission into measurable action across schools and communities in Sri Lanka.

Rich STEM Education

Conducting seminars, workshops, and academic guidance in Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics to help Sri Lankan students excel.

Financial Freedom

Equipping youth with practical knowledge, entrepreneurial thinking, and skills for sustainable financial independence and career readiness.

Positive Mindsets

Nurturing self-belief, leadership, character, and positive attitudes so young people can overcome challenges with resilience.

Community & Beauty

Fostering a compassionate society where empowered individuals actively give back and contribute to the beauty and fulfillment of their communities.
